HOMEGUARD HOMEOWNERS REFERENCE POLICY
Version: homeowners-reference-2.0 | Updated: 2026-09-15

Purpose
This is the specification for the proof-of-concept reference checks and synthetic
training labels. The random forest provides the application recommendation;
these checks provide label provenance, evidence controls, and disagreement holds.
They are illustrative project choices, not universal carrier standards, legal
requirements, actuarially validated thresholds, or an approved underwriting manual.
The original version is preserved in Git on the pre-upgrade backup branch.

Outcomes
A = STP ready for a next pricing step. No quote, bind, or issuance occurs.
B = Underwriter review. Resolve evidence/data issues or a risk question.
F = Recommended for rejection. Staff must review and own the final decision.
There is no automatic denial or adverse-action notice in this application.

Routing order
1. Normalize fields and recompute derived ratios from source amounts.
2. Predict A/B/F and class support with the versioned trained model.
3. Determine the risk reference class: any F reference finding -> F; otherwise
   any B risk finding -> B; otherwise A.
4. Any input/evidence/governance issue, class support below the configurable
   threshold (default 0.75), or model/reference disagreement -> final route B.
5. Otherwise retain the model recommendation. Model unavailability -> B.
A staff disposition is stored separately, never counted as automated STP.

Input quality (B holds)
All fields marked required in homeguard/schema.py must be present, including
keys entirely absent from a legacy record. Unknown, blank, null and NaN are
missing; they do not imply low risk. Numeric values must be finite and within
the schema ranges; integer fields must be whole numbers. Select values must be
supported options. Email, ZIP, and date formats are checked. ZIP is text.
Water/open claims cannot exceed total claims. Positive paid claims require a
nonzero claim count. Roof age cannot exceed dwelling age beyond a one-year
allowance; roof replacement year must agree within one year. A completed
verification date cannot be in the future.
The intake requires identifying fields and valid formats before saving, while
other missing underwriting data can be submitted for B review.

Evidence and governance (B holds)
Five checks must be Verified: property, roof, claims, hazard, and replacement
cost. Each requires a source reference. Verification also requires a reviewer
and a completed review date. Insured attestation and verification authorization
must both be Yes. A loss history with claims requires details. Reported living
area differing from verified area by more than 15% needs reconciliation.
External consumer-data use without governance documentation creates a B hold.
Legacy declarations of social-media, device, biometric, or credit-score inputs
create a governance review hold; none of these are model features.
These controls do not claim compliance with any jurisdiction's law.
A public non-demo submission cannot establish staff verification by selecting
Verified: those fields are reset to Not checked until staff reviews the record.
Synthetic examples may contain explicitly fictional completed evidence.

Claims / replacement cost (legacy field name: loss_ratio)
Computed as five-year paid claims / estimated dwelling replacement cost.
This is NOT incurred insurance losses / earned premium.
  below 0.25: no finding from this check
  0.25 through 0.75 inclusive: B
  greater than 0.75: F recommendation
A supplied loss_ratio field never overrides the recomputed value.

Claims history
  3 or more total claims in five years: F recommendation
  2 total claims: B
  Any open claim: F recommendation
  Any water-related claim: B

Roof
  Roof age over 25 AND reported condition Poor: F recommendation
  Roof age 20 or more, otherwise: B
  Reported condition Poor at any age: B
No roof imagery model or roof-AI confidence score is connected.

Hazards
  Wildfire score 50 through 84: B; 85 through 100: F recommendation
  Wind/hail score 50 through 79: B; 80 through 100: F recommendation
  Flood zone A, AE, AH, AO, V or VE: B
Scores are synthetic 0-100 POC indicators. No real provider scale is assumed.
State alone never sets a hazard score or model recommendation.

Occupancy and coverage
  Any known occupancy other than Owner-occupied: B
  Requested dwelling limit / replacement cost below 0.80: B
The 0.80 cutoff is an illustrative referral trigger, not an insurance-to-value
recommendation or a statement of a particular policy's settlement provisions.

Expanded questionnaire referrals (B)
  Active roof leaks or unrepaired damage
  Short-term rental, business activity beyond a home office, or structural work
  Trampoline, animal-bite/liability incident, prior flooding, or prior nonrenewal
  Aluminum or knob-and-tube wiring; fuse panel
  Galvanized or polybutylene plumbing
  No operational smoke alarms
  Pool with barrier not confirmed Yes; diving board or slide
  Supplemental solid-fuel stove without confirmed professional installation
  Condominium, manufactured home, or policy form Other / needs placement
  LLC / other entity ownership
  More than 30 consecutive unoccupied days or more than 30 days' coverage lapse
  Responding fire station farther than 5 miles
  Water heater older than 15 years
Other collected fields provide underwriter context, consent, or evidence;
collection does not mean every field is a trained predictive input.

Model development
Exactly 10,000 synthetic scenarios; seed 42; fixed scenario date 2026-09-15.
Reference label mix: 65% A, 25% B, 10% F. These are scenario design proportions,
not expected market outcomes or STP targets. The train/test split is stratified:
8,000 training and 2,000 held-out rows. Preprocessing fits training data only.
Model metadata records measured performance, parameters, features and checksums.
See docs/MODEL_CARD.md for scope and limitations.

Implementation map
homeguard/schema.py: field definitions and required responses
homeguard/validation.py: normalization, quality, evidence, reference findings
homeguard/model.py: training, artifact validation, prediction
homeguard/prediction.py: model plus holds and final recommendation
homeguard/storage.py: durable revisions, decisions, audit history and settings
homeguard/portfolio.py: deduplication, filters, current outcomes and metrics
The three legacy reference CSVs in data/ are retained for historical context;
they are not loaded as active legal, state, carrier, or behavioral decision rules.

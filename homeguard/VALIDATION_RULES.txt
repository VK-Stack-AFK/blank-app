HOMEGUARD UW VALIDATOR - VALIDATION RULES MATRIX
================================================================================
Generated: 2026-08-18
Version: 1.0
Coverage: All 50 US States

This document outlines all validation rules used in the HomeGuard UW Validator
system. Rules are applied consistently across all states and regions.

================================================================================
OVERVIEW
================================================================================

The validator routes applications into three categories:

  🟢 CATEGORY A (GREEN LIGHT) - Auto-Pass for Pricing
     Complete, low-risk applications ready for downstream processing
     Target: 70% of portfolio

  🟡 CATEGORY B (NEEDS UNDERWRITER) - Manual Review Required
     Moderate-risk or complexity requiring human judgment
     Target: 20% of portfolio

  🔴 CATEGORY F (AUTO REJECT) - Blocked for Compliance/Risk
     Applications blocked for regulatory, risk, or governance reasons
     Target: 10% of portfolio

================================================================================
LOSS RATIO RULES
================================================================================

Loss Ratio = Total Claims Paid (5 years) / Estimated Replacement Cost

Rule: Loss Ratio Threshold for Auto-Pass
  Condition: Loss Ratio < 0.25 (25%)
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY A (no rejection trigger)
  Rationale: Minimal historical claims indicate low loss exposure

Rule: Loss Ratio Threshold for Review
  Condition: Loss Ratio >= 0.25 AND Loss Ratio <= 0.75 (25-75%)
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B (flag for underwriter review)
  Rationale: Moderate claims activity warrants judgment call

Rule: Loss Ratio Threshold for Auto-Reject
  Condition: Loss Ratio > 0.75 (75%+)
  Action: TRIGGERS AUTO-REJECT to CATEGORY F
  Rationale: Excessive historical claims indicate unacceptable risk profile
  Legal: Basis for adverse action notice to applicant

================================================================================
CLAIMS HISTORY RULES
================================================================================

Rule: Excessive Claims Count (Auto-Reject)
  Condition: Prior Claims in Last 5 Years >= 3
  Action: TRIGGERS AUTO-REJECT to CATEGORY F
  Rationale: Three or more claims indicate pattern of loss
  Legal: Objective risk metric across all states

Rule: Moderate Claims (Review Flag)
  Condition: Prior Claims in Last 5 Years = 2
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B (flag for underwriter review)
  Rationale: Two claims warrant attention but not automatic rejection

Rule: Single Claim (Auto-Pass Eligible)
  Condition: Prior Claims in Last 5 Years <= 1
  Action: Does not trigger rejection; may support CATEGORY A
  Rationale: Single claim not indicator of pattern

Rule: Water Claim Pattern (Review Flag)
  Condition: Water Claims in Last 5 Years >= 1
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B
  Rationale: Water loss (roof leaks, burst pipes, etc.) may indicate maintenance
  Note: Combined with other factors to assess underwriter review need

Rule: Open Claims (Auto-Reject)
  Condition: Open/Pending Claims > 0
  Action: TRIGGERS AUTO-REJECT to CATEGORY F
  Rationale: Unresolved claims present legal/liability risk
  Legal: Cannot underwrite while claim is open

================================================================================
ROOF RULES
================================================================================

Rule: Roof Age (Auto-Pass Threshold)
  Condition: Roof Age < 20 years
  Action: Does not trigger rejection; supports CATEGORY A
  Rationale: Most roofs last 20-25 years; younger roofs indicate lower risk

Rule: Roof Age (Review Threshold)
  Condition: Roof Age >= 20 years AND Roof Age <= 25 years
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B
  Rationale: Approaching end-of-life; requires underwriter assessment

Rule: Roof Age (Auto-Reject Threshold)
  Condition: Roof Age > 25 years AND Roof Condition = "Poor"
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY F (when combined with other risk)
  Rationale: Very old + poor condition = high replacement risk

Rule: Roof Condition (Review Flag)
  Condition: Roof Condition = "Poor" OR Confidence < 60%
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B
  Rationale: Poor condition or low-confidence assessment warrants review

HAZARD EXPOSURE RULES
================================================================================

Hazard scores range 0-100, where higher = more exposure.

Rule: Wildfire Exposure (Review Threshold)
  Condition: Wildfire Score >= 50 AND < 85
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B (flag for underwriter review)
  Rationale: Elevated wildfire risk requires judgment on acceptability
  States: CA, OR, WA, CO, NM, MT, NV, UT, ID, WY

Rule: Wildfire Exposure (Auto-Reject Candidate)
  Condition: Wildfire Score >= 85
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY F (when combined with other risk)
  Rationale: Severe wildfire exposure may exceed carrier appetite
  Legal: Requires underwriter sign-off even on complete applications

Rule: Wind/Hail Exposure (Review Threshold)
  Condition: Wind/Hail Score >= 50 AND < 80
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B
  Rationale: Moderate wind/hail exposure warrants review
  States: TX, OK, KS, NE, SD, ND, LA (tornado/hail belt)

Rule: Wind/Hail Exposure (High Risk)
  Condition: Wind/Hail Score >= 80
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B (underwriter review recommended)
  Rationale: High exposure warrants manual assessment

Rule: Flood Zone (Review Threshold)
  Condition: Flood Zone = "A" OR "AE" OR "V" OR "VE"
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B
  Rationale: Special flood hazard zones require coverage decisions
  Legal: Standard homeowners excludes flood; separate NFIP coverage needed
  States: FL, LA, TX, NJ, NY, NC, SC (high flood exposure)

COMPLIANCE & GOVERNANCE RULES
================================================================================

Rule: Blocked Behavioral Factors (Auto-Reject)
  Blocked Factors: social_media_used, device_data_used, biometric_used
  Condition: Any blocked factor = "Yes"
  Action: TRIGGERS AUTO-REJECT to CATEGORY F
  Rationale: Privacy/proxy-discrimination risk; not allowed in this POC
  Legal: May violate fair lending laws; not supported by carrier

Rule: Maryland Credit Score (Auto-Reject)
  Condition: State = "MD" AND credit_score_used = "Yes"
  Action: TRIGGERS AUTO-REJECT to CATEGORY F
  Rationale: MD law prohibits credit-score-only refusal to underwrite
  Legal: State-specific compliance requirement (MD Ins. Code § 19-710.1)

Rule: NY External Consumer Data (Review Flag)
  Condition: State = "NY" AND external_consumer_data_used = "Yes" AND ai_governance_docs_ready != "Yes"
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B
  Rationale: NY AIS/ECDIS use requires documentation showing no unlawful discrimination
  Legal: NY Insurance Department guidance

Rule: Colorado External Consumer Data (Review Flag)
  Condition: State = "CO" AND external_consumer_data_used = "Yes" AND ai_governance_docs_ready != "Yes"
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B
  Rationale: SB21-169 requires controls against discrimination in algorithms
  Legal: Colorado state law

Rule: California External Consumer Data (Review Flag)
  Condition: State = "CA" AND external_consumer_data_used = "Yes" AND ai_governance_docs_ready != "Yes"
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B
  Rationale: CDI has warned on proxy discrimination/actuarial-nexus concerns
  Legal: California Insurance Commissioner guidance

Rule: Owner-Occupied Preferred
  Condition: Occupancy = "Owner-occupied"
  Action: No rejection trigger; supports CATEGORY A eligibility
  Rationale: Standard homeowners coverage scope

Rule: Non-Owner-Occupied (Review Flag)
  Condition: Occupancy = "Seasonal" OR Occupancy = "Investment"
  Action: Contributes to CATEGORY B

  Note: Standard policy may not apply; endorsements or different coverage needed

CATEGORY ROUTING LOGIC
================================================================================

CATEGORY A (GREEN LIGHT) - Requirements:
  ✓ All required fields present
  ✓ Loss ratio < 0.25
  ✓ Prior claims <= 1
  ✓ No open claims
  ✓ Roof age < 20 years
  ✓ Roof condition != "Poor"
  ✓ No compliance violations
  ✓ Owner-occupied
  ✓ No blocked behavioral factors
  ✓ No high-severity hazard exposure (wildfire <50, wind/hail <50, etc.)

CATEGORY B (NEEDS UNDERWRITER) - Criteria:
  • Loss ratio 0.25 - 0.75
  • Roof age 20-25 years
  • 2 prior claims (not 3+)
  • Water claim pattern
  • Moderate hazard exposure (wildfire 50-85, wind/hail 50-80, flood zones A/AE/V/VE)
  • Non-owner-occupied
  • Governance documentation missing (external data used)
  • Any other risk factors requiring human judgment

CATEGORY F (AUTO REJECT) - Triggers:
  ✗ Loss ratio > 0.75
  ✗ Prior claims >= 3
  ✗ Open claims present
  ✗ Blocked behavioral factors present
  ✗ State-specific compliance violations (MD credit score, etc.)
  ✗ Critical missing fields
  ✗ Very old roof (>25 years) + poor condition
  ✗ Severe hazard exposure (wildfire >=85, wind/hail >=80)
  [Any single trigger can auto-reject an application]

STATE-SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS
================================================================================

CALIFORNIA (CA)
  • High wildfire exposure (scores 50-100 typical)
  • Coastal flood risk in certain zones
  • External consumer data governance required (CA CDI guidance)
  • Applications with high wildfire often route to CATEGORY B or F

FLORIDA (FL)
  • Very high flood zone overlap (many properties in A/AE/V/VE zones)
  • High wind/hail exposure (hurricane risk)
  • Most applications have flood zone = not "X"
  • Special flood zone handling required

TEXAS (TX)
  • High wind/hail exposure (tornado/hail belt)
  • Diverse geography: west = wildfire, south = flood, north = hail
  • Large state with high volume

NEW YORK (NY)
  • Moderate hazard exposure (balanced)
  • AIS/ECDIS governance requirements (NY Insurance Department)
  • Significant application volume

MARYLAND (MD)
  • State-specific credit score prohibition (§19-710.1)
  • Credit score + any other risk = AUTO REJECT
  • Compliance requirement unique to MD

COLORADO (CO)
  • SB21-169 algorithm discrimination control requirement
  • Moderate hazard exposure (wildfire in western counties)
  • Governance documentation important

All Other States:
  • Apply consistent national rules
  • Adjust hazard exposure based on geography (see STATE_POPULATIONS data)
  • Use state-specific compliance rules only when applicable

================================================================================
IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
================================================================================

1. Rules are applied deterministically - same application always routes to same category
2. Rules are applied consistently across all 50 states (with state-specific compliance overlays)
3. Loss ratio is the primary driver of auto-reject decisions (>75% threshold)
4. Multiple moderate flags trigger category B, not auto-reject
5. Auto-reject triggers (loss ratio >75%, claims >=3, open claims, blocked factors)
   are sufficient on their own to reject, regardless of other factors
6. Governance flags (external data + no docs) contribute to category B, not auto-reject
7. This system is designed to be compliant with FCRA, ECOA, and state insurance laws
8. All adverse action decisions (category F rejections) should be communicated via formal
   adverse action notice within required timeframes
